What began as a photo-sharing app grew to become a robust platform suitable for business profiles, whether big or small. No longer are there boundaries based on size, because Instagram allows for businesses to raise brand awareness among new customers and maintain relationships with customers already in the picture, from international corporations to the small, local business on the corner. Here, I will take you on a step-by-step tour to switch the account from personal to business and reveal to you why you want to take the steps you've learned to transform the way you engage with your audience.

##### Why Instagram for Business?

**Reach Worldwide**

There are more than a billion Instagram active users worldwide. It is an ideal platform to showcase a product or service in the global market, with such a huge audience. The visual nature of Instagram allows businesses to communicate their brand message in a more engaging and relatable way. Be it a fashion brand wanting to showcase its newest collection or a restaurant looking to showcase your new menu items, Instagram is the channel created for creativity to reach out to customers who otherwise would be out of grasp.

**Brand Awareness**

One of the greatest advantages of [Instagram for business](https://zoftwarehub.com/products/instagram-business/overview) is building and growing brand awareness. While creating an identity, putting up great content, making sure whatever is being posted speaks out for your values and brand aesthetics will be put up. This can be done through visual aspects like color schemes, fonts, and imagery. Being a constant presence on Instagram can help the audience become familiar with the business and learn to trust and invest in you over time.

**Engaging with Customers**

Instagram is not a broadcasting website. While the platform does open a variety of tools to help businesses engage with their audience, that business just can't drive a one-way conversation. Features like Stories, polls, and direct messaging are just a few of the ways businesses can drive a two-way dialogue with their followers. It's this type of involvement that builds communities, fosters relationships, and eventually builds customer loyalty.

##### Why Upgrade to a Business Account?

**Instagram Insights Access**

Maybe the single most significant advantage of migrating to a business account is that it opens you up to using Instagram Insights. You get to learn the details of your followers and the performance of your posts. It includes data about the demographic and behavioral features of your audience and the times of maximum activity on the platform. Information like this is very useful in tailoring content to be the best for your particular audience, so your posts are most effective.

For instance, you'll know if most of your audience is active in the evening, meaning that is when you would schedule the posts for the highest engagement. It will also allow you to see what kind of content is doing great with your audience so you can go more in-depth into content that your audience loves.

**Running Ads**

Business accounts offer you the feature of running ads on Instagram. It will come in handy for stretching your reach to showcase your products or services to niche audiences. In fact, with the advanced ad-targeting options available on Instagram, businesses will be able to advertise only to users that fall under specific demographic, interest, or behavioral categories most related to their business.

You can further make multiple forms for your Instagram promotions. You can have several forms, including photo ads, video ads, carousel ads, and Story ads, to give you the freedom to select the one that you feel will perfectly articulate your desired advertising goals. Be it creating more awareness or getting people to your site to make purchases, Instagram forms have the ability to make that a reality.

**Adding Contact Buttons on Your Profile**

Allows for inclusions of contact buttons right in your Instagram profile. These buttons will allow potential customers to reach out to you more easily, be it through email, phone, or a physical address. This feature adds convenience, not only to your customers but also helps in building an infinitely more professional image of the brand.

For instance, a "Call" button will direct potential customers to your business line, while a "Directions" button will initiate the navigation to your storefront.

These are small but valuable details when it comes to enhancing the level of user experience and engaging interactions with your business.

**Preparing for the Migration Plan**

Before changing to a business account, you'll need to first critically look at your current Instagram profile. Check your profile picture: is it professional, and does it represent your brand? If not, you can always change it to something that reflects your business. The profile picture is the first impression the brand gets; therefore, make it count.

Secondly, review your bio. Your Instagram should give readers, clue them in what exactly your business is about, and what they can receive from it. It has to be short, interesting, and containing relevant keywords that will be used by users to search for the type of account yours is. Also, update your contact information and make sure it is located in an accessible place.

**Planning Your Content Strategy**

Switching to an Instagram [business account](https://zoftwarehub.com/products/instagram-business/overview) requires some strategic planning regarding the kind of content you will be sharing. Remember the types of content that really align with overall business goals. If you are looking at building brand awareness, you should, for example, prioritize visually appealing posts to highlight your products and services. If your main focus is driving sales, maybe you would wish to add more of your promotional content, along with some calls to action.

Think of how frequently you will post and the type of themes or topics you will be talking about. Be steady because that is the only way one gets followers on Instagram. Making the decision early reduces distractions on content that is not in line with your brand message.

##### Step-by-Step to switch your account

**Step 1: Launch Instagram and go to your profile space**

First of all, launch Instagram on your smartphone. Then reach your profile by clicking the profile icon located on the bottom right corner of your screen.

**Step 2: Get to the Settings**

On your profile page, click on the three horizontal lines present on the top right corner. A menu will appear, click on the last option in the menu named "Settings."

**Step 3: Get Account Settings**

Scroll down the Settings menu and tap "Account." This will bring up the page with all the account settings, where you can manage different settings for your Instagram account.

**Step 4: Switch to Professional Account**

Under account settings, you will get the option of "Switch to Professional Account." Click on this to start the process of converting an account from personal to business.

**Step 5: Set Business Category**

After that, it asks you to choose a category that describes your business the best. It can be anything from "Retail" to "Personal Blog", it totally depends on what your business is. The right category will help Instagram suggest your profile to people who might be interested in this kind of business.

**Step 6: Review and Confirm Your Contact Info.**

After you select your category, Instagram will ask you to confirm your contact information, that is email, phone number, and physical address in some cases. Make sure all that information is up to date and accurate. Then click "Done."

**Step 7: It's Official—You're Now a Business Account!**

You've just converted successfully to a business account! In your profile, you may notice some minor differences, such as the contact buttons, essentially found next to your profile picture, and access to Instagram insights.

##### Optimizing Your New Business Account

**Writing a Killer Bio**

Your Instagram bio is actually one of the most important components of your Instagram profile. Your bio is the very first thing people will see when they land on your page, so it's likely the thing that could make them stick around and read. A good bio makes it easy for people to know who you are, what you do, and what they can expect from your account.

So, if you were on the account of a bakery, your bio would go something like this: "Artisan bakery specializing in handmade pastries and organic breads. Visit us for daily fresh treats! ????????" This is a good bio in that it's highly descriptive and clear of what is expected to be found there, and it includes an emoji for a bit of fun and flair.

**Choosing a Professional Photo as Your Profile Picture**

Your profile photo should be something easily recognizable, like a logo or your company's mascot. If you're a personal brand, then go with a professional headshot. Either way, ensure it's of high quality and looks good in a small circle, because that's how it displays on Instagram.

**Leveraging Instagram Features to Increase Engagement**

Instagram has many features that can be useful for increasing both visibility and interaction. For instance, the Stories feature allows you to post content visible for just 24 hours. Great for sharing a lot more casual, behind-the-scenes stuff that perhaps isn't going to be polished enough for your main feed but still will be of value to the audience.

Other functions that you will want to capitalize on include IGTV and Reels. Use IGTV for more extensive tutorial or interview uploads; this can be beneficial for any, such as in a product demonstration. Reels can be engaging, short videos—really useful for quick tips or fun challenges that will show off your products being put into use.

Another feature many businesses will find helpful is Instagram Shopping. This allows you to tag products directly in your posts, allowing users to make purchases instantly without ever having to leave the app.

##### Transition Guidelines and Best Practices

**Consistency is Key**

Consistency with your content posting is what will keep your audience engaged. This does not simply mean posting frequently, though; it resonates down to the tone of voice and visual look you are using. This helps create a brand image that will resonate across an audience.

As a rule of thumb, it's good to post at least once daily. However, posting frequency may vary based on your audience's preferences and your content strategy. To know what time is best, use Instagram Insights to know the best time to make posts and maintain a regular schedule.

The main thing about social media is engagement. Look out for comments, messages, and mentions, and respond promptly. It will help you build relationships with your followers and get more visibility on the platform, as Instagram's algorithm will favor users who engage with their audience.

Even better, you might want to perform interactive media such as polls, quizzes, and questions on Instagram Stories. That way, you do not only capture your audience's attention but also enable detailed feedback and valuable insights.

**Use Your Hashtags Effectively**

Hashtags can help in making your content much more discoverable. Research and use the relevant hashtags that brand and align with your content. Do not just go for only popular hashtags; mix in some of those really nice niche hashtags catering to your specific audience.

Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post, but more often than not, it is recommended that one uses around 10 to 15 of the most relevant hashtags. Not too many generic hashtags should be used, as the message likely gets drowned in the sea of posts.

**Regularly Analyze Performance**

The best way to ensure that you are maximizing your [Instagram](https://zoftwarehub.com/products/instagram-business/overview) potential is to keep a watchful eye on your performance metrics using Instagram Insights. Check in often to see what posts are doing well, what time of day you are getting the most activity, and what type of post delivers better engagement. Use that information to continue to shape your strategy and optimize your content.

##### Issues and Solutions

**Feature Access Post-Switch**

Although the process of switching for the most part is very seamless, some glitches are sometimes experienced; for example, one is not able to access some features immediately. This is often subject to the taking of time by Instagram to fully process it. If you ever fall into such a trap, consider logging out of the app and then logging in. If the problem persists, you might also consider clearing your app cache or updating to the latest version.

**Lower Engagement Rates**

Indeed, some users have reported that engagement drops once they switch to a business account. This could be because of factors that vary from differences in the Instagram algorithm to the need to align more closely your content strategy with your business goals. So in case you find yourself clueless, take some time to browse your insights and experiment with various types of content to see what your audience actually loves.

**How to Reach Instagram Support**

For more highly technical issues that you could not solve on your own, feel free to get in touch with Instagram support. They can help you with problems you might have with your account features or anything else to do with ad management and all other technical stuff.
