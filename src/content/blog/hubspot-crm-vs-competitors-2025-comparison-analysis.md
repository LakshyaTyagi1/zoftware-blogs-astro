##### Pricing Overview

In **2025**, HubSpot CRM continues to offer a tiered pricing model that caters to businesses of all sizes and needs. The platform has a robust free version with essential CRM features, while paid plans include more advanced tools:

- **Free Plan:** Provides a solid foundation with contact and deal management, email tracking, live chat, and basic reporting.
- **Starter Plan:** Starts at $20 per month per seat ($15 per month if paid annually). It includes additional marketing, sales, and customer service tools.
- **Professional Plan:** Starts at $1,600 per month when billed annually, offering advanced features and higher usage limits.
- **Enterprise Plan:** Priced at $5,000 per month when billed annually, includes the most comprehensive features and customization options.
