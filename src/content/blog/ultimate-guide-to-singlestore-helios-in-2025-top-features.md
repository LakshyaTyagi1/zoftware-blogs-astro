##### Key Features and Benefits

**High-Performance Analytics and Workloads:** SingleStore Helios supports diverse data models such as JSON/BSON documents, vector search, full-text search, time-series, geospatial, and key-value, which are crucial for modern application requirements. Users benefit from 100-1,500x faster JSON analytics, millisecond query latency, and the ability to handle millions of upserts per second with infinite scalability.

**Elastic Scalability and Exceptional Availability:** Its architecture allows for seamless horizontal scaling and fault tolerance through the addition of aggregator and leaf nodes. This shared-nothing architecture ensures high availability and disaster recovery capabilities, making it an excellent choice for enterprise-level applications.

**Universal Storage Technologies:** The platform introduces Universal Storage technology, effectively merging the scan speed of a columnstore with the CRUD performance of a rowstore for operational analytics without the need for data movement.

**Separation of Storage and Compute:** This feature offers independent scaling of compute and storage resources, which results in cost efficiency and adaptability to varying workloads.
