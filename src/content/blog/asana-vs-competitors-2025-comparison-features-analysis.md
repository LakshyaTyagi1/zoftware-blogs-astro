##### Key Features and Benefits

**Comprehensive Suite of Features**

Asana offers a wide range of features designed to streamline project and task management, enhance collaboration, and improve productivity for teams of all sizes. Here are some of the standout features:

- **Task Automation:** Asana allows teams to automate repetitive tasks using rule-based automations. This not only saves time but also reduces the risk of errors, allowing teams to focus on high-impact work.
- **AI-Powered Workflows:** With the AI Studio, Asana enables teams to create smart workflows that adapt to evolving organizational needs. This includes triaging requests, suggesting timelines, and automating routine updates.
- **Multiple Views:** Asana provides various ways to visualize work, including lists, Kanban boards, timelines, calendars, and Gantt charts. This flexibility ensures that teams can organize tasks in the way that suits them best.
- **Custom Fields and Labels:** Teams can use custom fields and labels to sort, filter, and report on tasks with ease, providing greater control over project management.
- **Real-Time Reporting:** Actionable insights into project progress and team performance are available through real-time reporting dashboards, helping leaders make data-driven decisions.

##### Pricing Overview

**Flexible Pricing Plans**

Asana offers two main pricing plans to cater to different team sizes and needs:

- **Starter Plan:** Priced at $10.99 per user per month (billed annually) or $13.49 per user per month (billed monthly), this plan is ideal for growing teams that need structured collaboration. It includes timeline views, task dependencies, dashboards, workflow automation (up to 250 actions per month), advanced search filters, and custom templates. This plan supports up to 500 team members and provides tools for enhanced project tracking.
- **Advanced Plan:** Costing $24.99 per user per month (billed annually) or $30.49 per user per month (billed monthly), this plan is designed for larger teams requiring robust project management capabilities. It adds portfolio views, workload tracking, advanced reporting, 25,000 monthly automations, forms branching, proofing, and time tracking.
