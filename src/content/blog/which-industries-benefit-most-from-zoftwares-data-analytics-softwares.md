Most industries can gain from data analytics software, but some industries are more suitable for data science and analytics.

##### Retail

Retailers highly rely on big data and analytics to maintain customer loyalty. For instance, some retailers may utilize big data and analytics to generate personalized and meaningful buying experiences so that clients are satisfied enough.

##### Healthcare

Data analysis goes beyond certain diagnostic measures. It is used to personalize services for patients in the telehealth industry, to determine the effectiveness of certain medical products, and even in marketing. The technology can be used to improve patient care and experience. It makes patient history more accessible to medical practitioners, keeps track of patient health, and provides doctors with more data about their condition.

##### Finance

Banks and debit and credit card providers will always be on the lookout for fraudulent activity in your account. Data analysis and the predictive models built through it are excellent tools for determining whether fraudulent activity is taking place in a user's account. The same tech can be used to identify any gaps or security breaches.

##### Manufacturing

The manufacturing industry adds automation to its processes to guide production decisions, trend prediction, quality control and management, and even product development. Data analytics software now assists the sourcing of raw materials in certain industries, helping manufacturers stay prepared for any issues within the supply chain that might hinder their own operations.

##### Telecom

Media and entertainment usage has changed entirely due to data analytics and data analytics software. Most players have major OTT platforms that use data analysis to understand user needs.
