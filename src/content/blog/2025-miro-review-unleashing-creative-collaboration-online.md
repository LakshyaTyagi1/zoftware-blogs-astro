##### Pricing Overview

Miro offers a tiered pricing model, making it accessible to a broad range of users, from individuals to large enterprises. The free plan includes basic functions suitable for small teams or those at the initial stages of collaboration. The Team plan, priced at $8 per member per month (billed annually), unlocks unlimited boards and custom templates. The Business plan, at $16 per member per month, offers enhanced collaboration tools and unlimited guest access, while the Enterprise plan at $300 per user per year caters to larger organizations requiring advanced security and management features.
