##### Password Protection

The first defense behind keeping the secrecy of your documents involves passwords. A password added to your PDF ensures access into its contents is solely given to only authorized people and keeps the sensitive information from wrong hands.

It could be a business report, some legal contract, or personal information. Password protection is therefore to be included, and most of the documents require it on account of security purposes. Once you include a password on the document, you will be at the helm of who gets to see it, especially when sharing files via email or cloud platforms.

**How Sejda Does It:** With Sejda, one can easily add a password to his PDF. Open your PDF with the help of Sejda, click on the "Protect" icon, and from there select the option to "Add Password". Now enter the desired password, and the document shall be encrypted with the said password. No one can ever open that file again unless they have the password; therefore, only trusted recipients will ever read it.

##### Encryption of PDF Files

While documents are protected by passwords, encryption takes it a step forward. Encryption transforms the contents of the document into scrambled form which cannot be interpreted without the proper decryption key or password. It ensures that no unauthorized person can view or change the document even if it falls into their hands by chance.

It adds specifically to the data that is transferred via the internet and, which therefore might be intercepted or shared online. For businesses handling financial records, customer data, or proprietary information, encryption provides for the confidentiality and protection of the document.

**Sejda's Technique:** Sejda uses strong encryption techniques to PDFs. Thus, if a password is skipped, the content of the file is not-readable. For example, when a password is given to a file in Sejda, it encrypts the file with encryption, and then there will be another protection above the already given password.

##### Redaction of Sensitive Content

Sometimes, you may want to let someone know part of a document but cannot show other bits of information. This process of permanently removing sensitive information from a document is termed redaction. This might be personal identifiers, financial information, classified content, and so forth.

Various sectors, including health, legal, and finance, possess certain information that needs to be kept confidential. Thus, redaction of private content is a necessity before it is disseminated, ensuring that the privacy principle is complied with and that confidential data is kept safe.

**Using Sejda's Redaction Tool:** Sejda has made the confusion of redacting to null. It will be easy for you to select the text or images and parts that you want to mask from revealing, and Sejda will completely blackout the information. In the event that someone attempts to reverse the redaction, they are unable to access the hidden information, and your documents stay compliant and safe.

##### Digital Signatures

Digital signatures are becoming the norm for authenticating documents at a distance. More so, they affirm the identity of the signer and prove non-repudiation-proof-the signer has actually signed the document and will not deny it. Use where essential documents like contracts, agreements, or official letters come into play.

**Authenticity:** A digital signature confirms the authenticity of the document and ensures that no one has modified the document after its signing. This may be very important for documents that call for legal bindings or for any files requiring an official stamp of validation.

**Adding Digital Signatures in Sejda:** You can add digital signatures directly to the editor in Sejda. That way, you can actually digitize your signature without having to print, sign and scan back. Also, Sejda has secure digital signatures that are legally binding. Hence, these will make your documents safe and professional with high standards.

##### Watermarking Sensitive Documents

Watermarks are silent but potent forms of document labeling, ownership, or simply that the paper is confidential. The most basic types of watermarks can be some form of company logo or even a "Confidential" stamp reminding the recipient not to drop the paper.

**Why Watermarking Works:** Watermarking deters unauthorized sharing and traces back the origin of the document. In case sensitive documents are distributed by some individuals either unintentionally or intentionally, watermarks clearly state who the owner is in the first place so it can be traced far deeper in terms of security.

**Using Sejda to Watermark:** With the help of the watermarking feature provided in Sejda, you can add text or image watermarks to your PDF. You can brand your documents very fast and announce confidentiality. What's more, Sejda will let you define where to place a watermark, how opaque it should be, and its size so that it stands out but doesn't obscure your document content.
