**Explore Powerful Features for Enhanced Merchandising in 2025**

Ecommerce Product Merchandising provides a comprehensive set of features designed to optimize product placement, boost customer engagement, and drive conversions. Here’s what sets it apart in 2025:

- **Search & Category Pages:** Gain full control over how products appear on search and category pages. Arrange, pin, remove, or highlight products based on real-time data and overarching business goals.
- **Pinning Products:** Decide exactly what your customers see by pinning specific items to the top of pages. This feature allows you to highlight best-sellers, new arrivals, or high-margin products, giving you a strategic advantage in product presentation.
- **Removing Products:** Easily remove products from search results with just a few clicks. This functionality is ideal for managing seasonal inventory, discontinuing products, or maintaining curated selections. You can also quickly re-enable products to adapt to changing strategies.

- **Boost Rules:** The product’s 'boost rules' allow you to promote certain items based on their attributes, such as best-sellers, new arrivals, or high-margin items. This makes it easier to convert more customers by promoting products that matter most to your business.

- **Geo-Merchandising:** Use real-time targeting to dynamically display relevant results based on a shopper’s location.
