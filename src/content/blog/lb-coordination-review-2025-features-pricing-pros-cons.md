##### Key Features and Benefits

**Location-Based Planning:**

LB Coordination helps you work lean by dividing jobsites into zones and setting schedules for different trades in specific areas. This ensures maximum productivity with minimum downtime.

**Mobile Inspection:**

You can sync your teams on the cloud and generate turbo reports directly from the field to the office, greatly reducing paperwork and administrative workload. Mobile inspection makes it easier to deliver projects on time, within budget, and with real-time updates.

**Measure Project Performance:**

With planned vs. actual data visualization, you can easily monitor project performance, highlight non-working gaps, and make data-driven decisions to improve efficiency.

**Line of Balance:**

This feature enables parallel scheduling for repetitive tasks, helping break down silos by allowing trade groups to work in sync and make adjustments on the fly, whether filling scheduling gaps or reallocating resources.

**Cloud-Based Team Sync:**

LB Coordination offers cloud-based collaboration, ensuring your project stakeholders are always updated and aligned, regardless of their location.
