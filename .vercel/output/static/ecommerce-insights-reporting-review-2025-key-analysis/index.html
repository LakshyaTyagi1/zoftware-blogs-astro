Key Features and Benefits

Feature-Rich Analysis Tailored for 2025

Ecommerce Insights & Reporting offers a suite of features designed to give online retailers a 360-degree view of their ecommerce performance. From search analytics to product insights, the platform covers all crucial aspects of ecommerce reporting. Let’s explore the key features and benefits of this leading software in 2025:

  • Comprehensive Search Insights: Understand how shoppers engage with your site’s search engine using the powerful Search Insights Report. This tool enables you to analyze search patterns, refine merchandising, and make informed product recommendations, allowing you to cater effectively to customer preferences.
  • Actionable Reports: Ecommerce Insights & Reporting delivers a range of easy-to-pull, read, and analyze reports, including keyword analysis, sort options, expanded searches, overall search activity, zero-results scenarios, and filter usage. These insights help you quickly identify trends and areas for improvement, giving you a competitive edge in the market.
  • Product Performance Analytics: The platform offers detailed product insights, showing you which items generate the most views, revenue, and conversions. You can easily identify high-performing products and those that need optimization, enabling you to make data-driven decisions that drive sales and boost profitability.
  • Google Analytics Integration: Seamlessly integrate with Google Analytics to access high-level visualizations and breakdowns of your site’s search performance. This powerful feature allows you to gain a deeper understanding of shopper behavior and tailor your strategies accordingly.
  • Category-Specific Metrics: Ecommerce Insights & Reporting provides category insights to help you analyze how shoppers interact with different category pages on your site. Use this data to enhance the shopper experience and maximize conversions by optimizing category layouts and content.
  • Spell Correction Insights: The platform leverages years of search behavior data to identify and correct common spelling errors, ensuring that shoppers always find what they’re looking for. This feature improves the overall search experience, reduces frustration, and enhances customer satisfaction.

These advanced features are designed to help you stay ahead of the competition by enabling smarter merchandising, targeted product recommendations, and optimized site performance in 2025.
