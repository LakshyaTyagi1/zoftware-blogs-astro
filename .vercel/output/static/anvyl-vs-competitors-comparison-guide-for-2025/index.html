Key Features and Benefits

Comprehensive Supply Chain Management

Anvyl comes packed with top-tier features, making it a versatile solution for supply chain management in 2025. Its primary offering is centralizing supply chain data from various sources, giving businesses a unified view of their operations. This centralization helps in making informed decisions quickly, reducing the time required for data collection and analysis.

  • Automated Milestone Tracking: Keeps track of all significant project milestones automatically, providing real-time updates to help businesses stay on top of their supply chain processes without manual intervention.
  • Payment Tracking: Streamlines financial management by integrating payment tracking into the supply chain workflow, allowing businesses to manage their finances more efficiently within a single platform.
  • New Project Collaboration: Enhances team collaboration by providing robust tools for planning, communication, and coordination, leading to improved project outcomes and faster execution.
  • Custom Tasks and Full Audit Trail: Offers customizable task management and a comprehensive audit trail to ensure transparency, accountability, and compliance within the organization.
  • Centralized Document Management: Facilitates easy storage, sharing, and retrieval of important documents, promoting better information management and collaboration among team members.
  • Logistics Visibility: Provides real-time insights into logistics and order management, helping businesses optimize their supply chain operations and make informed decisions.
  • Order Delay Risk Analysis: Uses advanced analytics to predict and address potential delays, mitigating supply chain risks and ensuring smooth operations.
  • 3-Way Matching: Enhances accuracy and compliance in order fulfillment by verifying orders against receipts and invoices, a critical feature for financial control and inventory management.
  • Smart Milestones and Review Reports: Offers advanced milestone tracking and detailed review reports, giving businesses deeper insights into project progress and overall supply chain performance.
