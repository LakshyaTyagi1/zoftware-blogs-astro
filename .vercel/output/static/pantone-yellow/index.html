Pantone Yellow: Meaning and Significance

Mar 12, 2024
• 8 min read

Pantone is a standardized color matching system that is used across many industries, including graphic design, fashion, and product manufacturing. It is a universal language of color that allows designers, brands, and manufacturers to communicate and reproduce specific colors accurately. The Pantone system contains over 1,800 colors, each with a unique identification number, making it easy to match colors across different mediums and materials.

One of the most popular colors in the Pantone system is yellow. Yellow is a bright and cheerful color that is often associated with happiness, positivity, and energy. In the Pantone system, there are several shades of yellow, each with its unique identification number and name. Understanding the meaning and symbolism behind yellow in the Pantone system can help designers and manufacturers choose the right shade for their projects and products.

The Pantone Yellow Chart is a helpful resource for anyone looking to use yellow in their designs or products. It contains all the different shades of yellow available in the Pantone system, along with their identification numbers and names. By referring to the Pantone Yellow Chart, designers and manufacturers can easily choose the right shade of yellow for their specific needs.

Understanding Pantone

Pantone is a standardized color matching system used in various industries, including graphic design, printing, and fashion. It is a universal language for color that allows designers, manufacturers, and clients to communicate and reproduce colors accurately.

Pantone colors are identified by a unique number or name and are created using a specific mix of pigments. The Pantone Matching System (PMS) is the most commonly used Pantone system, which consists of over 1,800 colors. Each color is assigned a unique number, making it easy to identify and reproduce.

Pantone colors are used in a variety of ways, including:

  • Design: Designers use Pantone colors to choose the exact shade they want for their designs, ensuring consistency across different mediums and materials.

  • Printing: Printers use Pantone colors to ensure that the printed product matches the intended color. Pantone colors are particularly useful in situations where color accuracy is critical, such as in branding and packaging.

  • Fashion: Pantone colors are used in the fashion industry to create color palettes and ensure consistency across different materials and products.

Pantone Yellow is a popular color in the Pantone system. It is a bright, vibrant yellow that is often used in branding and marketing materials. Pantone Yellow is identified by the number PANTONE 116 C and is a popular choice for companies looking to create a bold, eye-catching look.

Overall, Pantone is an essential tool for anyone working with color. It provides a standardized system for identifying and reproducing colors accurately, making it easier for designers, manufacturers, and clients to communicate and collaborate effectively.

The Meaning of Yellow in Pantone

Yellow is a bright and cheerful color that is often associated with happiness, optimism, and positivity. In Pantone, yellow is a popular color that is used in a variety of applications, from branding to product design.

Pantone has a variety of yellow shades, each with its unique meaning and symbolism. Here are a few of the most popular yellow shades in Pantone and what they represent:

  • Pantone Yellow C: This bright and bold shade of yellow is often associated with energy, enthusiasm, and excitement. It is commonly used in advertising and marketing to grab attention and convey a sense of urgency.

  • Pantone Yellow 012 C: This shade of yellow is a bit more subdued than Pantone Yellow C, but it still conveys a sense of warmth and positivity. It is often used in branding and product design to create a friendly and approachable image.

  • Pantone Metallics 871 C: This shade of yellow is a bit different than the others, as it has a metallic finish. It is often used in high-end product design to convey a sense of luxury and sophistication.

In addition to these specific shades, yellow in general is often associated with a variety of positive emotions and traits. For example, yellow is often used to represent:

  • Happiness

  • Optimism

  • Creativity

  • Warmth

  • Friendliness

  • Playfulness

 Whether you are looking to create a bold and energetic image or a warm and friendly one, there is a shade of yellow in Pantone that can help you achieve your goals.

Pantone Yellow Chart

The Pantone Yellow Chart is a color guide that features different shades of yellow created by Pantone Inc. The chart is used by designers, artists, and manufacturers to ensure color consistency in their work.

The Pantone Yellow Chart includes several shades of yellow, each with a unique code. The most commonly used shades are Pantone Yellow C and Pantone Yellow 012 C. Pantone Yellow C is a bright, sunny yellow, while Pantone Yellow 012 C is a slightly darker shade with a touch of green.

Overall, the Pantone Yellow Chart is an essential tool for anyone working with the color yellow. It provides a standardized way to communicate and reproduce the color, ensuring consistency and accuracy in design and production.

Different Shades of Pantone Yellow

Pantone Yellow is a bright and vibrant color that can evoke feelings of happiness, warmth, and optimism. Several different shades of Pantone Yellow vary in their hue, saturation, and brightness. Here are some of the most popular shades of Pantone Yellow:

  • PANTONE Yellow C: This shade of yellow is one of the most popular and recognizable shades of Pantone Yellow. It is a bright and sunny yellow that is often used in advertising and branding. PANTONE Yellow C has a hex code of #FFD900 and an RGB value of 255, 217, 0.

  • PANTONE 116 C: This shade of yellow is a lighter and more subdued version of PANTONE Yellow C. It is often used as a secondary color in branding and design. PANTONE 116 C has a hex code of #FEDD00 and an RGB value of 254, 221, 0.

  • PANTONE 1235 C: This shade of yellow is a warmer and more orange-toned version of PANTONE Yellow C. It is often used in packaging and product design. PANTONE 1235 C has a hex code of #FFB81C and an RGB value of 255, 184, 28.

  • PANTONE 102 C: This shade of yellow is a lighter and more pastel version of PANTONE Yellow C. It is often used in fashion and interior design. PANTONE 102 C has a hex code of #F9D857 and an RGB value of 249, 216, 87.

  • PANTONE 137 C: This shade of yellow is a darker and more mustard-toned version of PANTONE Yellow C. It is often used in industrial and automotive design. PANTONE 137 C has a hex code of #EAAA00 and an RGB value of 234, 170, 0.

These are just a few of the many shades of Pantone Yellow that are available. Each shade has its unique characteristics and can be used to convey different moods and emotions. 

Conclusion

Yellow Pantone shades have been used throughout history to convey various meanings and symbolism. From joy and warmth to caution and warning, the color yellow has a rich cultural significance.

Pantone Yellow C, with its vibrant and sunny hue, has become a timeless color that radiates positivity, creativity, and joy. It is a popular choice in fashion and interior design, as well as in branding and marketing.

Designers and manufacturers can find Pantone Yellow C in various Pantone products, including the Pantone Formula Guide and Solid Guide Set. The color can be used in combination with other shades, such as Ultimate Grey, to create a thoughtful and optimistic atmosphere.
