Introduction

Key Features and Benefits

Planview AgilePlace in 2025 offers a comprehensive suite of features and benefits that drive agile program management at scale:

  • Agile Program Management: Make planning and delivery easier with agile program management. Planview AgilePlace helps in steering multiple teams towards common objectives while promoting autonomy within those teams.
  • Collaboration and Notifications: Foster collaboration across teams with instant notifications and updates. These features are designed to enhance communication, ensuring everyone stays informed and aligned.
  • Teams of Teams Planning: Plan and manage work at various levels by connecting teams to programs and value streams. This helps enterprises streamline large-scale projects without losing sight of strategic objectives.
  • Work Planning and Delivery: Create, manage, and deliver work faster with robust planning and delivery tools that keep workflows optimized and output consistent.
  • Enterprise Integrations: Seamlessly connect with other popular tools like GitHub, Jira, Zapier, Microsoft Visual Studio, and Oracle Primavera Cloud to streamline your workflow.
  • Cross-team Work Connections: AgilePlace brings together diverse teams, departments, and processes, thereby aligning more closely with business strategies.
  • Advanced Reporting Capabilities: Gain real-time insights into your operations with advanced reporting and analytics. This includes Lean metrics that help identify bottlenecks and improve processes.
  • Digital Kanban Boards: Visually track and manage work with customizable Kanban boards. These boards can be adapted to your team’s unique processes, making them suitable for both small groups and large enterprises.
  • Process and Work Visualization: Get a clear overview of tasks, bottlenecks, and progress. This visibility is crucial for eliminating waste and accelerating delivery.
  • Complete Process Mapping: Map out own team-defined processes to promote continuous improvement and optimize workflows for increased efficiency and speed.
  • Kanban Board Templates: Enable teams to quickly set up and customize Kanban boards with predefined templates for various workflows and processes.
  • Real-time Work Status: Keep stakeholders up-to-date with real-time work status updates, promoting transparency and smarter decision-making.
  • Identify Risks to Delivery: Quickly pinpoint bottlenecks and risks that may prevent on-time delivery. Adjust resource allocation to keep projects on track.
  • Work in Process (WIP) Limits: Set WIP limits to avoid overburdening teams and ensure a consistent, manageable flow of work.

Key benefits:

  • Promotes continuous flow and delivery
  • Reduces bottlenecks and dependencies
  • Enables cross-team planning and collaboration
  • Improves resource allocation and risk management
  • Supports customized team workflows
  • Provides real-time work status and analytics
  • Empowers teams with Lean metrics and insights
