Financial planning software for financial advisors

Feb 23, 2024
• 8 min read

Financial planning software has become an indispensable tool for financial advisors aiming to deliver accurate and personalized advice to their clients. The complexities of modern finances demand precision and adaptability that manual calculations and traditional methods cannot offer. 

With these technologically advanced platforms, advisors can streamline their workflow, foster compliance, and enhance their decision-making processes. In an era where data rules, financial planning software empowers advisors by aggregating client data into a single, accessible location. 

Overview of Financial Planning Software

Financial planning software is a comprehensive tool for financial advisors to develop tailored financial plans and manage client portfolios more efficiently.

Purpose of Financial Planning Software

Alt Text - Infographic of buyers investing in financial planning software in an increasing order mentioning the total fund investment of 3,616.9 million USD for year 2021. 

Since 2012, the global market for financial planning software has grown above 3 million USD. Looking at the digital revolution more and more companies and financial advisors are investing in technology to improve the efficiency of their work. 

Additionally, as per the research, we will see an increase in financial advisors investing in proficient and robust financial planning software by 16.3%. 

Financial planning software helps financial advisors streamline the process of creating, implementing, and monitoring a personalized financial plan for their clients. Its primary objectives include:

  • Enhancing Accuracy: Reducing the risk of human error in financial plan calculations.

  • Time Efficiency: Saving time on tasks such as data entry and analysis, thereby allowing advisors to focus on client engagement and strategy.

Key Features of Financial Planning Tools

Financial planning software typically includes the following key features that aid advisors in their services:

  • Cash Flow Analysis: Tools for projecting future cash flows and assessing client financial health.

  • Investment Management: Capabilities for tracking and optimizing investment portfolios.

  • Retirement Planning: Modules for forecasting retirement needs and resources.

These tools help in demonstrating the potential outcomes of various financial strategies, allowing advisors to convey complex financial concepts to clients in a clear manner.

Common Software Platforms

The financial advising landscape features several software platforms that cater to different advisory needs. Commonly used platforms include MoneyGuidePro, eMoney Advisor, and NaviPlan. Each platform provides distinct functionalities suited for varied advisory approaches and client demographics.

Client Management Solutions

Financial planning software provides comprehensive tools designed to streamline client management processes for financial advisors.

Client Data Aggregation

Client Data Aggregation entails systematically collecting and consolidating clients’ financial information from various sources. These solutions offer:

  • Real-time Data Syncing: Immediate updates from banks, investment portfolios, and other financial institutions.

  • Account Categorization: Sorting of assets and liabilities for a well-organized financial overview.

Client Relationship Management

Client Relationship Management (CRM) systems are pivotal in managing interactions and maintaining a history of communications with clients. Key features include:

  • Contact Management: Storage of detailed client profiles with contact information, financial goals, and personal notes.

  • Activity Tracking: Record of meetings, calls, emails, and task creation with reminders for follow-ups.

Client Portal Functions

Client Portal Functions serve as a secure, digital gateway enhancing client engagement and self-service capabilities. Efficient client portals provide:

  • Document Sharing: Encrypted transmission and storage of sensitive financial documents.

  • Interactive Tools: Financial calculators, performance dashboards, and goal-tracking modules.

Financial Analysis and Reporting

Financial planning software equip financial advisors with tools to analyze, forecast, and report on their clients’ financial health accurately and efficiently.

Cash Flow Analysis and Forecasting

To manage a client’s finances effectively, understanding cash inflows and outflows is crucial. Financial planning software offers robust cash flow analysis features that allow advisors to track past transactions and predict future ones based on various scenarios. These tools often include:

  • Monthly cash flow tables that itemize recurring income and expenses

  • Projection graphs to visualize future cash flow under different conditions

Investment Planning Tools

Financial advisors use investment planning tools to streamline portfolio management and make informed decisions. Key features include:

  • Asset allocation analysis to optimize investment strategies

  • Real-time market data integration for up-to-date financial decision-making

Customizable Reporting Capabilities

Customization in reporting enables advisors to present data in a way that aligns with individual client needs and preferences. Financial planning software typically offers:

  • Report templates for standard financial statements

  • Ad-hoc report creation allowing for personalized reports with specific metrics and visualizations

Portfolio Management and Tracking

Financial planning software offers sophisticated tools to financial advisors, streamlining the portfolio management process with features that support asset allocation, real-time monitoring, and compliance.

Asset Allocation Strategies

Financial advisors employ software to define and implement asset allocation strategies that align with individual client goals. The software can manage many asset classes, aiding in diversifying and balancing client portfolios. Tools within the software often include:

  • Risk assessment modules to match client risk profiles with appropriate investments.

  • Model portfolios: allowing for the quick application of pre-set allocation strategies.

Real-Time Portfolio Monitoring

Advisors can monitor client portfolios with real-time data, which is crucial for adaptive management and prompt decision-making. Features include:

  • Live market feeds to update asset values continuously.

  • Alerts and notifications: set for specific market events or performance thresholds.

Compliance and Regulation Adherence

Software solutions help advisors navigate and adhere to the complex regulatory environment. They include

  • Regulatory reporting tools to simplify the creation and submission of required documents.

  • Audit trails: which record all changes and transactions to assist with regulatory reviews and compliance checks.

Integration with Other Systems

Financial planning software for financial advisors must seamlessly integrate with various systems to enhance functionality and streamline financial management processes.

Accounting Software Compatibility

Financial planning software must be compatible with leading accounting software to facilitate real-time financial data synchronization. This ensures financial advisors maintain a coherent financial picture and make informed decisions based on up-to-date information.

  • Examples of Compatible Accounting Software:

    • QuickBooks

    • Xero

    • Sage 50cloud

Tax Preparation Integration

Direct integration with tax preparation software is critical for accurate and efficient tax planning. It allows advisors to automatically transfer client financial data, which helps in the preparation of tax returns and optimization of tax strategies.

  • Key Tax Software Integrations:

    • TurboTax

    • H&R Block

    • TaxAct

Third-Party Application Syncing

Financial planning tools need to sync with third-party applications to provide a comprehensive suite of services. This includes data feeds from investment platforms and aggregation with customer relationship management (CRM) systems.

  • Third-Party Syncing Includes:

    • Investment Platforms: Morningstar, eMoney Advisor

    • CRM Systems: Salesforce, HubSpot CRM

Implementation, and Training

Financial advisors seeking to integrate financial planning software will find the transition smoothest when they follow a structured onboarding process, have access to comprehensive training materials, and receive ongoing support for software upkeep.

Onboarding Process

The onboarding process for financial planning software typically begins with an initial setup phase. During this phase, financial advisors must:

  • Configure system settings to align with their firm’s operational procedures.

  • Import existing client data into the new system, often facilitated by data migration tools provided by the software vendor.

  • Integrate with other platforms, such as CRM systems or portfolio management tools, to ensure seamless data flow across applications.

Training Resources and Support

Vendors often offer a wealth of training resources and support options to ensure financial advisors can effectively leverage their software. Such resources include:

  • Live training sessions: Webinars or in-person workshops.

  • Self-paced learning modules: Online tutorials, videos, and documentation.

  • Dedicated support: Phone, email, or live chat assistance from trained professionals.

Software Updates and Maintenance

To maintain software efficiency and security, vendors perform regular updates and maintenance. Financial advisors can expect:

  • Scheduled updates: Periodic enhancements and new features.

  • Security patches: Ongoing protections against emerging threats.

  • Technical support: Assistance with resolving any software issues that arise.

Security and Data Protection

In financial planning software for advisors, security and data protection are paramount due to the sensitivity of client financial data. Financial advisors depend on robust security measures to safeguard against data breaches and ensure client confidentiality.

Encryption Standards

Financial planning software utilizes Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) with 256-bit keys to protect data in transit and at rest. This encryption method is widely recognized as secure and is used by federal agencies to protect classified information. Vendors typically employ Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) or Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocols for data transmission security.

Data Backup and Recovery

The software incorporates redundant data backup systems in multiple locations to provide resilience against data loss. A standard practice includes nightly backups and the ability to restore data to any point within the last 30 days. Advisors can expect:

  • Automatic backups, preventing data loss due to human error.

  • Quick recovery options to minimize downtime in case of a disaster.

Access Control and Authentication

Access to financial planning software is tightly regulated. Administrators have the ability to monitor logins, set password complexities, and enforce regular password changes, maintaining the integrity of the system’s access protocols.

Pricing and Subscription Models

Financial planning software for financial advisors typically offers diverse pricing and subscription models to accommodate various business sizes and needs.

Cost Structure

Most financial planning software providers structure their pricing per-advisor or per-client basis. Some may offer a flat monthly fee, while others could have a tiered pricing model that scales with the number of clients or the depth of services provided. 

Free vs Paid Features

Free Features:

  • Basic financial analysis tools

  • Limited client accounts management

Paid Features:

  • Advanced analytics

  • Customizable client reports

  • Secure client portals

  • Integration with other financial tools

Providers often differentiate their services by offering essential functionalities for free, with advanced features reserved for paid subscriptions.

Upgrades between subscription tiers are generally seamless, with the added cost proportional to the increase in clients or features. Some software may provide customizable packages where additional features can be added à la carte to existing plans.

  • Customizable templates

Future Trends and Developments

Financial advisors can expect significant enhancements in software capabilities and industry compliance standards.

Emerging Technologies

Emerging technologies are poised to revolutionize financial planning software. Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning algorithms are leading the charge, providing more accurate predictions and personalized advice. 

Regulatory Changes Impacting Software

Regulatory bodies are continually updating compliance requirements, which financial planning software must adhere to. A current focus is on data protection, as seen with regulations like the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in the United States. Financial planning software must evolve to ensure financial advisors can effectively manage and protect client data.

Another regulatory aspect is the transparency in financial advice itself. One can expect features that help illustrate potential conflicts of interest and the fee structure for advisory services, a direction fostered by evolving fiduciary standards.

Industry Adoption and Growth Projections

Large firms are leading in adopting sophisticated software, while smaller firms and independents are catching up. Banking, wealth management, and insurance are the leading adopters by sector. The drive towards digital transformation, fueled further by the pandemic, encourages more firms to invest in and integrate financial planning software into their practice, hinting at a robust growth trajectory ahead.

Conclusion 

Financial planning software is crucial in empowering financial advisors to optimize client outcomes. By leveraging advanced technology, these tools facilitate comprehensive analysis, personalized recommendations, and streamlined communication, enabling advisors to offer tailored solutions that align with their clients’ goals and objectives.
