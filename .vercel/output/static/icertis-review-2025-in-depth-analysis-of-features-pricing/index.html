Introduction

Looking to manage contracts smarter in 2025? Icertis is here to help you transform how your enterprise handles contracts. Icertis Contract Intelligence is an innovative contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform designed for the enterprise segment.

Icertis gives companies the power to digitize, store, and intelligently manage all contracts in the cloud. By using advanced artificial intelligence, it helps users spot problematic clauses, ensure compliance, and turn contracts from static documents into a strategic advantage. Enterprises in 2025 need more than just a storage solution for contracts—they need software that can extract actionable insights from complex legal documents and make contract management a core part of their operations.

Icertis’ key strengths include: industry-defining AI-powered contract intelligence, seamless scalability, and integration capabilities that suit rapidly changing organizational needs. Its dedicated focus on compliance, particularly with regulations like GDPR, further underscores its relevance in today’s regulatory landscape.

Available at https://www.icertis.com/, Icertis is positioned as a market leader in contract intelligence and lifecycle management for the enterprise in 2025.

Key Features and Benefits

Icertis Contract Intelligence offers a robust platform packed with features tailored for enterprise-level contract management in 2025. Here are the highlights:

  • Buy-Side (Suppliers): Icertis helps manage supplier contracts efficiently, allowing enterprises to maintain better supplier relationships and ensure compliance with supplier agreements. This feature is particularly useful for businesses with complex supply chains.
  • Completion Tracking: The platform offers real-time tracking of contract completion, ensuring that all stakeholders are aware of the contract status. This feature helps in timely execution and reduces delays in the contract management process.
  • Compliance Tracking: Icertis provides advanced compliance tracking tools, helping organizations stay aligned with regulatory requirements such as GDPR. The platform’s compliance tracking capabilities are essential for businesses operating in highly regulated industries.
  • Contract Lifecycle Management: Icertis delivers a comprehensive solution that covers all stages of the contract lifecycle, from initiation and execution to renewal and termination. This end-to-end approach ensures that no aspect of contract management is overlooked.

Additional Features:

  • Data Extraction: Leverage machine learning to extract key data points from contracts and support analytics.
  • Pre-built Templates: Simplify contract creation with customizable, pre-built templates for common contract types.
  • Team Collaboration and Workflow Management: Enable efficient collaboration across departments with integrated workflow management and real-time updates.
  • Version Control and Electronic Signatures: Ensure smooth negotiation and approval processes with robust version control and integrated e-signature functionalities.
  • Approval Process Control: Manage contract approvals with customizable workflows and audit trails.
  • CRM and Specialty Contracts: Integrate with CRM systems and manage specialty contracts with ease.

Icertis adopts a flexible pricing model designed to cater to the diverse needs of large enterprises:

  • The platform primarily offers a custom quote-based enterprise pricing model. Interested organizations can contact the Icertis sales team to get a tailored quote based on their requirements and the scale of their operations.
  • Add-on modules: Additional modules like Icertis Obligation Management, Icertis Risk Management, and Icertis NegotiateAI are available as optional add-ons. These modules enhance the core functionalities of the platform, allowing businesses to customize their contract management solution further.
  • Usage-based pricing: Icertis also provides usage-based pricing, depending on the selected add-on modules. This approach ensures that organizations only pay for the features they need, making it a cost-effective solution for businesses of varying sizes and needs.
  • Flexible billing: Customers can choose between monthly or annual billing cycles, offering greater flexibility in managing their budgets and contract management expenses.

Industry Applications

  • Technology and IT Services: Manage complex software licensing agreements, service-level agreements (SLAs), and vendor contracts.
  • Healthcare and Life Sciences: Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and manage clinical trial agreements, supplier contracts, and more.
  • Financial Services: Handle high-value contracts, regulatory compliance, and risk management for banking, investment, and insurance sectors.
  • Manufacturing and Supply Chain: Streamline supplier management, track contract performance, and maintain compliance across global supply chains.
  • Legal and Professional Services: Automate the creation, negotiation, and management of various legal contracts and service agreements.

Thanks to its modular design and customizable workflows, Icertis can be tailored to meet the specific needs of any industry. The platform’s advanced compliance tracking and risk management features make it particularly valuable for organizations operating in highly regulated environments.
