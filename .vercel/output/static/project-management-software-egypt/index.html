Egypt’s Project Control: A Close Look at the Leading 6 Project Management Softwares

Mar 7, 2024
• 8 min read

As per Wellingtone’s study of 111 companies in 29 countries, 25% needed the right tools for project management, which caused delays and disturbances in their performance. 

Despite the size and nature of companies, poor project management always leads to loss. 

In Today’s blog, we are going to talk about Egypt project management softwares. 

According to Egypt Today, “In 2018, Egypt surpassed Saudi Arabia as the second largest single projects market in the region, recording more than $33 billion of contract awards.”

Such a fast-developing country must use the right project management software in Today’s tech-driven business environment. Hence, we have some fantastic project management software for your business. 

6 Leading Project Management Software
  1. Nutcache 

Overview

Nutcache is a collaborative project management software designed to enhance productivity in projects. With its real-time budgeting, it ensures that it’s aligned with your financial targets. It helps in avoiding financial issues. 

Nutcache’s integrated billing module provides accurate invoices for work and expenses. This helps streamline all payment processes. Also, It allows smooth integration with other tools for those who have an established invoicing system..

Features

  • Real-time budget tracking: Nutcache allows teams to track project budgets in real-time. It helps in analyzing the project’s financial status. This prevents overspending and enables proactive adjustments, too. 

  • Integrated billing module: With this module, Nutcache has simplified the billing process. It allows users to invoice billable hours and expenses. This integration ensures that all billable work is accurately invoiced.

  • QuickBooks:  if you are using QuickBooks, Nutcache integrates it to sync financial data. This ensures consistency across platforms and streamlines the accounting process.

  • Zapier integration: With Zapier, Nutcache can connect to many other apps and services. It automates the workflows and facilitates data exchange between different systems. 

  • Collaborative environment: Nutcache allows team members to collaborate with each other. Also, it provides tools for sharing, managing, and communicating in real time. 

 Pricing:

Pro: $12/user/month/billed yearly

Enterprise: $21/user/month/billed yearly

Corporate: Customized plan

  1. TOPdesk

Overview

TOPdesk project management software allows multiple projects on a single platform. This enhances communication, collaboration, and performance in the project. Also, TOPdesk enables you to overview your project phases in graphs. 

With it, you can customize your project timelines and make mid-project adjustments. Also, TOPdesk is adaptable to different workflows without any hassle.

Features

  • Unified platform: TOPdesk consolidates all project-related activities into one platform. This reduces the need to switch between different tools and improves efficiency.

  • Customizable project timelines: TOPdesk allows the creation of custom timelines for each project. Also, it is flexible enough to add new phases or milestones as projects evolve.

  • Graphical project overviews: It provides a visual representation of project phases and schedules. This helps teams to understand what needs to be done and when. Also, promote better planning and execution.

  • Dependency tracking and phase assignment: TOPdesk enables the assignment of dependencies and time frames for each project phase, ensuring that sequential tasks are completed correctly.

  • Budget tracking and cost analysis: Project members can log hours and costs for individual tasks, while project managers can view total costs by phase or for the entire project, aiding in budget management and analysis.

 Pricing:

Customized plan

  1. Screendragon

Overview

Screendragon provides a comprehensive solution for high-performing teams and streamlines operations across projects. It provides a centralized hub for managing projects and budgets. 

Screendragon’s project management software provides a functional integrated interface. Such an integrated interface eliminates switching between different tools and modules.

Features

  • Centralized operations hub: Screendragon offers a single point of control for all project-related operations, aiming to eliminate the inefficiency of disconnected tools.

  • Integrated processes and budget management: The platform integrates project processes with budget management, providing a comprehensive view of both operational and financial aspects.

  • Designed for marketing and professional services: Screendragon is specifically tailored to meet the needs of marketing teams, agencies, and professional service firms, with features and workflows suited to these sectors.

  • User-friendly interface: The software boasts a beautifully designed interface that is intuitive and easy to navigate, which can enhance user adoption and satisfaction.

  • Streamlined project and resource management: By centralizing project management software activities, Screendragon makes it easier to manage resources and processes, leading to more efficient project execution.

 Pricing:

Customized plan

  1. ProofHub

Overview 

ProofHub is a project management solution that simplifies the way teams work together. It brings all your project management needs under one roof. With ProofHub, you can plan, collaborate, organize, and deliver projects on time. 

ProofHub also allows all project-related activities under the central hub. It ensures that everyone from the team to stakeholders is integrated and informed.

Features

  • Centralized Projects and Tasks: Organize and manage projects of any size from a single platform, enhancing clarity and collaboration.

  • Custom Roles and Fields: Tailor the platform to your project’s needs with customizable fields and user roles, ensuring relevant data is captured and access is controlled.

  • Multilingual Interface: Work in your preferred language, supporting multiple languages making it inclusive for diverse teams.

  • White Labeling: Customize the look of your ProofHub account to align with your brand, offering a consistent user experience.

  • Time Tracking and Reports: Keep projects on schedule with integrated time tracking and detailed reports to monitor progress.

 Pricing:

Ultimate Control: $89/user/month/billed yearly

Essential: $45/user/month/billed yearly

Overview

ClickUp project management software stands out for its adaptability and interface. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools that cater to various work requirements. It provides a single app for teams to manage tasks, docs, goals, and chat, among other things. The software focuses on customizable features and tailoring the platform to their needs. 

Also, ClickUp keeps updating its software by adding new features for practical work. 

Feature

  • Centralized Projects and Tasks: Organize and manage projects of any size from a single platform, enhancing clarity and collaboration.

  • Custom Roles and Fields: Tailor the platform to your project’s needs with customizable fields and user roles, ensuring relevant data is captured and access is controlled.

  • Multilingual Interface: Work in your preferred language, supporting multiple languages making it inclusive for diverse teams.

  • White Labeling: Customize the look of your ProofHub account to align with your brand, offering a consistent user experience.

  • Time Tracking and Reports: Keep projects on schedule with integrated time tracking and detailed reports to monitor progress.

  1. JIRA by Atlassian

Overview 

JIRA by Atlassian is a powerhouse in the agile project management space. It is tailored for software development teams and supports the agile methodology. This allows teams to plan, track, and release software with high efficiency. 

It integrates with other tools in the Atlassian suite and third-party apps. Making it a versatile choice for teams that want to maintain a single source of truth for their projects.

Features

  • Agile Planning: Break down complex projects into manageable tasks with user stories, issues, and customizable workflows.

  • Extensive Integrations: Connect seamlessly with a wide range of tools and add-ons from the Atlassian Marketplace.

  • Customizable Templates: Jumpstart your projects with ready-made templates for Scrum, Kanban, and more.

  • Advanced Reporting: with detailed reports and analytics, gain insights into your team’s performance.

  • Scalability: Despite being a startup or a large enterprise, JIRA scales to meet your needs without friction.

Standard: $8.15/user/month/billed yearly

Premium: $16/user/month/billed yearly
