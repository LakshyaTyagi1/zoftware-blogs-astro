Key Features and Benefits

Execute Performance offers an impressive selection of features designed to meet the unique needs of large enterprises in 2025. Here are some key highlights:

  • Customizable Templates: Build customized templates for different groups in your organization, such as by role or department. This allows you to tailor the review process for various teams, ensuring that each group’s unique competency requirements are addressed.
  • Integrated Goal Reviews: Align employee performance with company objectives by integrating goals and OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) directly into the review process. This helps keep everyone focused on strategic priorities.
  • Flexible Workflow: With highly configurable workflow and rule configuration tools, organizations can design their performance management process to run differently for various teams, departments, or units.
  • Competency Model Integration: Use the built-in competency model or import your own. This flexibility enables organizations to drive best practices for employee development and growth.
  • 9-Box Assessment: Effortlessly evaluate employee potential and performance using the intuitive 9-box assessment tool, making talent management and succession planning more efficient.
  • Customizable Workflow for Reviews: Execute Performance supports alternative workflows for organizations that want to conduct 30-60-90 day reviews or check-ins for new hires. You can also tailor review schedules based on work anniversaries or other milestones.
  • Comprehensive Development Support: Encourage employee growth by aligning development initiatives with organizational values and competencies.

With Execute Performance, you can cultivate a culture of continuous feedback and support, making monthly or quarterly check-ins, peer feedback, and actionable insights easily accessible for everyone.
