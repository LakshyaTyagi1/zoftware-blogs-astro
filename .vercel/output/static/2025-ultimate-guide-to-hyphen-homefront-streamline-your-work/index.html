Introduction

In 2025, amidst the rapidly evolving landscape of the construction industry, Hyphen HomeFront stands out as a cloud-based enterprise resource planning (ERP) solution tailored specifically for home builders. As part of the Hyphen Solutions suite, this innovative software product has revolutionized the way builders manage sales contracts, budgeting, communications, and more, offering a centralized platform for tracking customer selections, change orders, and documents with unparalleled efficiency. Its fully integrated system encompasses the entire build process – from sales and CRM to service and warranty – eliminating the need for multiple software solutions.

With its robust feature set and positive impact on operational efficiency, Hyphen HomeFront has garnered acclaim for supporting residential construction management, seamless system integration capabilities, comprehensive features, and ease of use. As the go-to ERP for mid to large-size home builders, it is the ultimate tool for streamlining workflows, reducing build times, and enhancing communication across construction stages in 2025.

Key Features and Benefits

Comprehensive Integration: Hyphen HomeFront unites various aspects of the home building process into a single, efficient system, including CRM and sales management, project scheduling, and document management. Its integration fosters improved efficiency and communication across different stages of construction.

Technology and Flexibility: Deployable both online and on-premise, Hyphen HomeFront provides flexibility to accommodate the specific needs of the business, ensuring that pricing and features align perfectly with the scale and requirements of the user’s operations.

User-Centered Design: Emphasizing a user-friendly interface, the ERP makes navigation and utilization accessible for all skill levels, supporting a positive user experience that’s consistently praised by its user base.
