Sun Smart Global offers nTireCRM, a leading CRM software in Oman. This software solution is designed to manage customer relationships and their databases seamlessly. Features 

  • Intelligent Business Dashboards: nTireCRM provides enriched, intelligent business dashboards and graphical reports, ensuring real-time visibility of all CRM activities.

  • Comprehensive Lead Management: The software offers features like Lead Management, Cross Sell/Up Sell, Lead Stage, Nature, Priority, and Rating Configurations. It also supports auto lead generation based on lead profiles and other product profiles.
