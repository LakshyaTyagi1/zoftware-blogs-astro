Introduction

In 2025, the pace of software development continues to accelerate, pushing teams to seek solutions that enhance delivery speeds while ensuring the quality and integrity of their applications. Split, now an integral component of the Harness suite of tools, stands at the forefront of this evolving landscape. Available at Harness’s Feature Management & Experimentation platform, Split has redefined how development teams manage feature flags, conduct experiments, and deliver software efficiently. With its comprehensive platform, Split enables controlled feature releases and data-driven decision-making, ensuring that organizations can innovate rapidly without compromising on performance or user experience.

Key Features and Benefits

Split’s platform is distinguished by its robust feature management and experimentation capabilities, designed to accelerate software delivery and minimize risks. The key features and benefits include:

  • Enterprise-Scale Feature Flagging: Create, target, and manage feature flags that allow for granular control over who sees what features, enabling safer, incremental rollouts.
  • Automated Performance Tracking: Automatically track errors and performance issues tied to each feature flag, streamlining issue detection and response.
  • Real-Time Data-Driven Decision Making: With Split’s A/B testing and experimentation tools, teams can make informed decisions about product changes without needing additional resources.
  • Seamless Integration: Split integrates with a plethora of development tools and systems, enhancing productivity and workflow efficiency.

In essence, Split provides a unified solution that supports a culture of innovation and psychological safety among development teams, as fully detailed on Split Features Page.
